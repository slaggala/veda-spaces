# Customer-promise matrix: Essential Specification v1.1

**Source:** [`essential-1.1-promise-matrix.json`](essential-1.1-promise-matrix.json). Tests: `api/tests/unit/test_promise_matrix.py` (every visible specification statement is listed, category by category, and every link of the chain is filled in) and `app/scripts/staging-build.test.mjs` (every page statement is on the page and every fixed page promise is in the matrix).

**Status:** the operational chain is **PROPOSED**. Operations confirms each verification step and its owner before ESSENTIAL-1.1 is activated. A promise that cannot be verified is removed or rewritten as a final-selection statement.

Chain: estimator promise → specification version → detailed quotation → procurement → installation → handover → warranty source.

## Material categories

### structure

- **Customer wording:** Cabinet and furniture structure: Branded plywood furniture. Cabinet and furniture bodies in branded plywood.
- **Internal requirement:** Carcass, shelf, back-panel and TV-background boards in plywood of the approved grade (October 2026 baseline: Sylvan Blu): 18 mm bodies, 9 mm backs, 12 mm TV backgrounds. Vanity and utility boards to be confirmed by sales (wet areas).
- **Approved brand examples:** Sylvan Blu
- **Equivalent rule:** Approved brands such as the named examples, or an approved equivalent of the same grade and specification.
- **Quotation mapping:** Every carcass or body line (kitchen base, wall and loft; wardrobe and loft; TV box, storage and panel; crockery; study; vanity; bed; utility; storage boxes; window seat; laminate partition) names board brand, grade and thickness.
- **Procurement verification:** Purchase order and supplier invoice name brand, grade and thickness; goods received checked against the board stamp and the quotation.
- **Execution verification:** Site supervisor checks board stamps and thickness before fabrication or installation; photographs kept with the project record.
- **Handover evidence:** Handover record lists board brand and grade with the invoice reference; manufacturer warranty card where the manufacturer issues one.
- **Warranty source:** Manufacturer’s documented terms for the exact product named in the quotation; Veda Spaces workmanship and fitment service per the policy (one year from handover).
- **Accountable role:** Designer (quotation mapping); project manager (procurement and site checks)
- **Status:** PROPOSED: operations to confirm each verification step and owner before activation
