| Proposed behavior | Governance classes: STANDARD_CONTROL < DUAL_CONTROL (holds a sensitive permission) < FOUNDER_GOVERNANCE. Any change that raises a target's class (GRANT_FOUNDER, role or permission change) withdraws in the same transaction everything authorised under the weaker class: open STANDARD requests are cancelled (TARGET_BECAME_FOUNDER) and a pending email proposal not requested by the owner is cleared with its links (EMAIL_CHANGE_CANCELLED, reason TARGET_BECAME_FOUNDER or TARGET_BECAME_PRIVILEGED, masked address, class transition). At verification the proposal's authorising class (self, direct administrator, EMAIL_CHANGE request, FOUNDER_EMAIL_CHANGE request) is recomputed against the target's current class; a weaker authorisation, or a DISABLED account, fails closed with EMAIL_TOKEN_INVALID and EMAIL_CHANGE_VERIFIED FAILURE (plus FOUNDER_GOVERNANCE_BYPASS_BLOCKED for Founders). Founder-level requests re-validate the action against the current target at execution (INVALID_STATE → FAILED). |
