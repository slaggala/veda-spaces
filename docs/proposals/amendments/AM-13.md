| Proposed behavior | Governance classes: STANDARD_CONTROL < DUAL_CONTROL (holds a sensitive permission) < FOUNDER_GOVERNANCE. Withdrawal happens on GRANT_FOUNDER and on user-level role or permission changes that raise the class: open STANDARD requests are cancelled (Founder) and a pending email proposal not requested by the owner is cleared with its links (EMAIL_CHANGE_CANCELLED with reason and class transition). A class raised in any other way — a role DEFINITION gaining a sensitive permission — does not withdraw anything: the proposal is only BLOCKED at verification while the class stays raised, and if the change is reverted the old link verifies again (FC-08). At verification the proposal's authorising class (self, direct administrator, EMAIL_CHANGE request, FOUNDER_EMAIL_CHANGE request) is recomputed against the current class; weaker authorisation or a DISABLED account fails closed (EMAIL_TOKEN_INVALID; EMAIL_CHANGE_VERIFIED FAILURE; FOUNDER_GOVERNANCE_BYPASS_BLOCKED for Founders). Not covered (FC-A09): administrator-issued password-reset links and MFA-enrollment links survive a promotion to Founder; they are delivered only to the account's own verified address. Founder-level and break-glass requests re-validate the target at execution. |
