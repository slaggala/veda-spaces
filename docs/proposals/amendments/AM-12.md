| Proposed behavior | A deleted Founder keeps FOUNDER protection and roles and is restored only by FOUNDER_STATUS_CHANGE status=RESTORE: requested by one eligible Founder, approved in-app by a second, distinct eligible Founder (requester and target can never approve; one decision per request). The account returns DISABLED and FOUNDER-protected; reactivation is a separate FOUNDER_STATUS_CHANGE ACTIVE. Eligibility is checked at request, approval and execution under the governance lock; at execution the target must still be deleted and its address free, otherwise the request FAILS. Neither break-glass channel (single-Founder or custodian) may carry a restore — it is outside the approved break-glass policy (SECOND_FOUNDER_REQUIRED). POST /users/{id}/restore applies G3 and G11 and answers 403 FOUNDER_PROTECTED with a FOUNDER_GOVERNANCE_BYPASS_BLOCKED event for a Founder. Last-Founder invariants are re-checked (a restore never reduces the Founder count). Precise behaviour (FC-A10, FC-A11, FC-A12): the custodian CLI cannot address a deleted account at all — it refuses the restore as an unknown target and records no BREAK_GLASS_REQUESTED FAILURE event; SECOND_FOUNDER_REQUIRED is the service-level answer for in-app and direct callers. The FOUNDER_ACTION_REQUESTED/EXECUTED/FAILED events carry action FOUNDER_STATUS_CHANGE; RESTORE appears in the request payload (status) and in the FOUNDER_TRANSITION event. A restored and later reactivated Founder signs in with the password and MFA factor held before deletion (deletion revoked sessions and links, not credentials); the owner may require a FOUNDER_MFA_RESET and a password reset as part of reactivation. |
