| Existing certified behavior | FOUNDER_STATUS_CHANGE status ∈ {DISABLED, ACTIVE, UNLOCK, DELETE}; restore of deleted users is USER-001 (user.restore) with G9 only. |
| Proposed behavior | A deleted Founder keeps FOUNDER protection and roles and is restored only by FOUNDER_STATUS_CHANGE status=RESTORE: requested by one eligible Founder, approved in-app by a second, distinct eligible Founder (requester and target can never approve; one decision per request). The account returns DISABLED and FOUNDER-protected; reactivation is a separate FOUNDER_STATUS_CHANGE ACTIVE. Eligibility is checked at request, approval and execution under the governance lock; at execution the target must still be deleted and its address free, otherwise the request FAILS. Neither break-glass channel (single-Founder or custodian) may carry a restore — it is outside the approved break-glass policy (SECOND_FOUNDER_REQUIRED). POST /users/{id}/restore applies G3 and G11 and answers 403 FOUNDER_PROTECTED with a FOUNDER_GOVERNANCE_BYPASS_BLOCKED event for a Founder. Last-Founder invariants are re-checked (a restore never reduces the Founder count). |
| Reason | Owner decision OD-2: a single Founder must not reverse a dual-control deletion. |
