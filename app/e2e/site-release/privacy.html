Privacy notice

Notice version 2026-09-v1 · Website enquiries and the online budget estimate

This notice explains what the Veda Spaces website collects when you send an enquiry or use the online budget estimate, why, and the choices you have. It is the notice the enquiry form refers to when it asks for your consent.

1. When you send an enquiry

The enquiry form asks for your name and phone number, and optionally your email address, project location, property type, the service you are interested in, a budget range and a short description of your requirements. We use them to respond to your enquiry and, with your consent, to contact you about it.

Together with the form we record: the version of this notice you agreed to and the time you agreed; the page the form was on; the network address the form was sent from, kept as evidence that the consent was yours; and, where your visit came from a campaign link, the campaign parameters in that link and the page that referred you. These help us understand how people find Veda Spaces; they are never used to profile you.

If you have used the online budget estimate, the enquiry can carry its reference so that the design team sees the choices you made. The estimate itself holds no personal data.

If the form cannot reach our systems, it offers to continue on WhatsApp with the details you typed; WhatsApp's own terms apply to that conversation.

2. Your consent and how to withdraw it

The form sends nothing until you tick the consent box. You can withdraw your consent at any time by telling the Veda Spaces team (by phone, WhatsApp, email or in person). After that we stop contacting you about the enquiry; the record of your original consent and of its withdrawal is kept as evidence. Withdrawing consent does not affect a project already agreed with you, whose own terms apply.

3. The online budget estimate

Where the online estimate is offered, it works without any personal data: the rooms, sizes and preferences you choose are sent to our systems to calculate a preliminary budget range, and the result is kept under a random reference for a limited validity period so you can refer to it. An estimate that is never attached to an enquiry is removed after its retention period. Your browser keeps your choices for the session only, so you can go back and change them. Where a newer, catalogue-based version of the estimate is offered, it may also send Veda Spaces anonymous counts of the rooms and options people look at. Those counts carry no name, choices or contact details, are not reduced to one per session, and are not sent when your browser's Do Not Track setting is on.

4. How we measure the website

Where it is switched on, the website sends Veda Spaces a small number of anonymous counts: that a page was visited, that the budget estimate was started, that the enquiry form was started, and that this journey page was read. Each count is sent once per browser session, using a random token your browser keeps for the session only. The counts hold no name, address, page history, device details or network address, are not combined with your enquiry, and cannot identify you. The website sets no cookies for this, and it sends nothing when your browser asks not to be tracked.

5. Who sees your enquiry and how long it is kept

Your enquiry is seen by the Veda Spaces team members who handle enquiries and projects. It is kept while your enquiry or project is active. A closed enquiry has its personal data removed after a retention period that Veda Spaces sets; the business records of a project are kept as the project's terms require. You can ask Veda Spaces to correct your details or to erase the personal data of an enquiry that did not become a project.

6. Questions about your data

For any question about this notice, your details or your consent, contact the Veda Spaces team. Keep your enquiry reference handy if you have one.

Notice version 2026-09-v1. It describes what the website does today; a changed notice carries a new version, and the form asks for consent to the version it shows.
