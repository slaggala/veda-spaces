What happens after you choose Veda Spaces

Choosing an interiors partner is a decision about the months that follow. This page explains how Veda Spaces runs a home interiors project from the first conversation to the care after handover: what happens at each step, what you receive, and what we will ask of you. Nothing here is a quotation or a guarantee; your quotation and its terms are the documents that apply to your project.

1 Consultation

It starts with a conversation about your home, how you live, what you need and what you have in mind for the budget. You can begin with the enquiry form, a call or WhatsApp. If you have used the online budget estimate, bring its reference: we review it with you rather than starting again.

What we ask of you: your requirements and priorities, and a time when we can see the home.

2 Site measurement

A Veda Spaces surveyor visits and measures the home: every room in scope, walls, openings and electrical points. The measurements are recorded and verified, and they become the basis for the scope and the quotation. The visit is arranged with you in advance.

What we ask of you: access to the home at the agreed time, and any drawings you already have.

3 Design and scope

From the measurements we work out the scope with you: which rooms and items are included, the materials and finishes, and what stays outside the work. The scope is confirmed in writing before it is priced, so the quotation describes exactly what you discussed.

What we ask of you: your choices and confirmations, in writing, so nothing is assumed.

4 Quotation

You receive a written quotation that lists the rooms and items, what is included and excluded, the assumptions it rests on, taxes shown separately, the payment schedule and the warranty and service terms that apply. A quotation is prepared only after the measurement and the confirmed scope. The online budget estimate, where it is offered, is a preliminary range and never a quotation.

What we ask of you: read it fully, and ask about anything that is unclear before you decide.

5 Your decision

Questions and change requests are answered before you decide; a changed scope means a revised quotation, not a verbal promise. Your acceptance is recorded in writing against the exact version you accepted. From then on the accepted scope is fixed, and any later change is priced and agreed separately as a variation. If you decide not to proceed, telling us is enough; there is no obligation.

6 Project onboarding

Your project is booked against the accepted quotation and your Veda Spaces project team is named. The design details and the scope are finalised and frozen, materials and hardware are ordered against that frozen scope, and the site is checked for readiness before installation is scheduled.

What we ask of you: the site-readiness items that depend on you, agreed in advance, so installation starts on the planned date.

7 Payments and documents

The payment schedule in your quotation ties each instalment to a stage of the project. Each payment is invoiced and receipted, so you always know what has been paid and what remains. Your quotation, invoices and receipts are the documents of record.

8 Execution

Production, procurement, site coordination and installation follow the frozen scope. Work is planned in packages, and you are told where the project stands and what comes next. If something pauses the work, you are told what it is and whose action it needs.

9 Quality checks

Finished work is checked in detail: fitment, finishing and alignment, recorded check by check. A failed check is corrected and rechecked before the work is treated as complete.

10 Snags

Anything you or we notice that is not right is raised as a snag, tracked until it is fixed, rechecked and closed. Tell your Veda Spaces contact about anything you notice and it is recorded as a snag; a snag is never closed without the fix being verified.

11 Handover

Handover is a walkthrough of the completed work with you, with a handover package that records what was completed, the checks and the snags that were resolved. You acknowledge it, and the warranty and service period begins from the handover date.

12 Warranty and service

The warranty on materials and hardware and the Veda Spaces workmanship service are set out in the Warranty, Service & Customer Care Policy, which forms part of your quotation. It includes one year of free service from the project handover date for fitment-related or workmanship issues within the completed Veda Spaces scope. A service request is recorded, assessed against the policy, and resolved with a visit where one is needed.
